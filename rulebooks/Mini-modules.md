# Nature Mini-Modules

## Elephant Graveyard

Trigger ability: When a population is killed for any reason: +1 meat food to the elephant graveyard.

- Species with Scavenging eat 1 meat food after a hunt instead of 2.
- Species with Scavenging may forage or hunt to eat 1 meat food from the elephant graveyard.

### Solo Rules

If any of Rani's species have Scavenging, they will eat from the elephant graveyard only after all other options have been exhausted.
