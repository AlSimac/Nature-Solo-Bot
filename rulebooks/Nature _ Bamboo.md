# Bamboo Grove

Bamboo `[bamboo]` is a new type of food that a species can eat to keep a population alive. However, species cannot score bamboo `[bamboo]` unless allowed otherwise.

## Setup

Set up Nature as normal, with these additions:

1. Shuffle the Bamboo Grove cards to form a face-down deck next to the Nature deck.
2. Place the `[bamboo]` tokens next to the watering hole.

## Add Food

Instead of adding `[plants]` to the watering hole, add a number of `[bamboo]` to the watering hole equal to the number of `[plants]` you would add normally. No plants are added during Preparation when playing with this module.

## Draw Cards

During Preparation, you will draw your five cards in turn order from any of the available decks. For example, you might draw three Nature cards and two Bamboo Grove cards, or perhaps all five cards from the Bamboo Grove deck. You may not look at your new cards until you have drawn all five. If any deck runs out while drawing cards, shuffle the respective discard pile to create a new draw deck.

## Hidden Hunters

In addition to the Hunter cards from Nature, there is one trait in Bamboo Grove that allows you to become a hunter; Stalking. This trait has this icon `[flip to hunter]`, indicating to flip the size dial to the hunter side when the trait is revealed. Flip the size dial back to the forager side if all traits with `[flip to hunter]` are removed from the species.

## Feeding Phase

When a species takes the forage feeding action, it has the options of either eating plants `[plant]` OR eating bamboo `[bamboo]` from the watering hole, equal to its size plus any additional amount indicated by its traits. The same forage feeding action cannot be used to take a mix of `[bamboo]` and plants. Previously eaten food (`[plants]`, `[meat]`, or `[bamboo]`) cannot be removed from a population (to make space for a different food type, for example).

## Free Action

When discarding a Bamboo Grove card, add bamboo `[bamboo]` equal to the value shown on the discarded card. When discarding a regular Nature card, add plant `[plant]` as usual. (This is the only common way `[plant]` can enter the watering hole when playing with this module.)[a][b]

## Scoring

Species cannot score `[bamboo]` unless allowed otherwise. Immediately before Scoring, discard all `[bamboo]` from each of your species that cannot score it.

Place any scored `[bamboo]` behind your player screen, as you would with any other food type. They are each worth 1 point at the end of the game.

Population fed with a food type it cannot score still survives, as the food is removed after the end of the Feeding Phase (after checking for starvation).

## Traits

### Key Words

- Evasive trait: Any trait that can block a hunter from targeting the species in question, in ways other than providing defense value `[shell]` — for example Fast from Nature, Arboreal from this module, or Camouflaged from Flight.

### Monophagous

Can only score `[bamboo]`. Do not discard `[bamboo]` before scoring from a species with Monophagous, however discard any other food type from such a species.

### Folivore

- A species with Folivore adds 1 to its defense value `[shell]` when being hunted.
- Folivore triggers when any of your other species forages (even if that species has Folivore). When Folivore triggers, the species eats 1 `[plant]`, directly from the supply.

### Arboreal

- A species with Arboreal adds 1 to its hunt value `[claw]` when hunting.
- A species with Arboreal can only be hunted by a species with Arboreal (or by one that can otherwise ignore this trait, such as Stalking).
- A species with Arboreal eats 1 additional food of the same type from the same source when eating. When foraging from the watering hole, it would eat 1 additional plant `[plant]` or bamboo `[bamboo]` from the watering hole. When hunting another species, it would eat 1 additional meat `[meat]` from the supply.

### Stalking

- A species with Stalking is a hunter. Its size dial `[red]` will show the hunter side to remind you that it can no longer take the forage feeding action.
- Each time when targeting a fully fed species, the species with Stalking can ignore one evasive trait of the target species (see definition above).

### Root Eating

After a species with Root Eating forages (whether eating plant `[plant]` or bamboo `[bamboo]`), eat or discard (return from the watering hole to the supply) one plant `[plant]` or one `[bamboo]` from the watering hole. It can be a different food type than the one the species originally foraged for. One of the two options must be resolved (if any food remain in the watering hole after the foraging), so if you do not wish (or cannot) eat any of the remaining food from the watering hole, you must discard one of them.

## FAQ

### When playing with Bamboo Grove and Natural Disasters, what happens to events that refer to plants `[plant]` in the watering hole?

Any event affecting plants `[plant]` specifically in the watering hole (or their placement during Preparation) affects bamboo `[bamboo]` instead.

### When playing with Climate or Arctic Tundra, how are the rules of Preparation affected by the rules of Bamboo Grove?

Determine the number of plants `[plant]` that would be added according to the rules of any other module in play, but then place that amount of bamboo `[bamboo]` instead.

### When an effect creates a fed population (faraway place in Flight, Fertile trait in Amazon Rainforest, etc.) for a species with Monophagous, what happens?

Take a bamboo `[bamboo]` from the supply instead of the usual meat `[meat]`, so that it can be kept during Scoring.

### Can Migratory Grazing (Arctic Tundra) or Seasonal Migration (Climate) be used to eat bamboo `[bamboo]` from the supply?

No, you may only choose to eat bamboo `[bamboo]` instead of plants `[plant]` when foraging from the watering hole. Any effect specifically targeting plants `[plant]` only target plants.

### Long Digestion

Immediately before scoring, when bamboo `[bamboo]` would be discarded from the population of a (non-Monophagous) species with Long Digestion, move it to the Long Digestion trait card instead. If the trait is still in play at the end of the game, the player scores 1 point each for all the bamboo `[bamboo]` on the trait (as if they were behind their player screen).

If the trait is discarded at any point, or the species goes extinct, all bamboo `[bamboo]` on it are discarded to the supply.

### Territorial

- During Preparation, each round, place 2 plants `[plant]` or 2 bamboo `[bamboo]` (player’s choice) from supply onto each Territorial trait a player has in play on their species.
- During Feeding Phase, a forager `[green]` species with Territorial may target their own Territorial trait card(s) to forage from instead of the watering hole, using the usual rules of foraging.
- Any food not taken stays for next round (and another 2 food will be added per Territorial trait). Any food still present at the end of the game is discarded, not scored.

## Solo

### Adapting Phase

- If the Monophagous trait would be added to a species that cannot forage, skip this species; if a Monophagous species can no longer Forage, discard its Monophagous trait.

### Feeding Phase

- When feeding a species that cannot score Bamboo, Rani will discard a non-bamboo card for food in preference to a bamboo card; when feeding a species that can score Bamboo, Rani will discard a bamboo card for food in preference to a non-bamboo card.
- Rani will discard cards to create plant food for a species that cannot score bamboo in preference to eating bamboo.
- Rani will only forage for Bamboo if she can feed more population by doing so (be sure to consider all traits!)
- A species with Monophagous will prioritise eating Bamboo over any other type of food.
- Root-eating: Rani will eat rather than discard if one of this species' population can be fed, otherwise Rani will discard; Rani will discard Plants before Bamboo.
- Stalking: Rani will ignore ALL evasive traits when hunting a fully fed species. This is her advantage.
